Gryffindor

Gryffindor values bravery, courage, and determination. Its members are known for standing up for what is right, even in the face of danger.

Slytherin

Slytherin is associated with ambition, leadership, and resourcefulness. Students in this house are driven and often strive for greatness.

Ravenclaw

Ravenclaw values intelligence, creativity, and wisdom. Its members are known for their love of learning and problem-solving abilities.

Hufflepuff

Hufflepuff emphasizes loyalty, patience, and hard work. Members are fair-minded and dedicated, valuing kindness and teamwork.
